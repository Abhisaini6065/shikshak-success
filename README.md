# Shikshak Success – Future Teacher Shivani

Welcome to **Shikshak Success**, a preparation platform for the UP Super TET Primary Level examination.

## Features
- Subject-wise practice questions
- Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
- Multiple-choice quizzes
- Five answer options where applicable
- Interactive test experience
- Mobile-friendly design
- Instant answer feedback

## Subjects
Preparation content for the major subjects included in the UP Super TET Primary Level syllabus.

## How to Use
1. Open the website.
2. Select your subject.
3. Practice the questions.
4. Choose your answer and check your result.
